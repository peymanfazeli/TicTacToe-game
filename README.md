# How To Play

## Goal

Win the round by placing three of your marks in a row before the system does.

You can win with:

- Three marks across a row
- Three marks down a column
- Three marks diagonally

## Choose Your Side

Before the round starts, choose whether you want to play as **X** or **O**.

- X always takes the first turn.
- If you choose X, you play first.
- If you choose O, the system plays first.

## Choose Level

Choose how strong the system should be:

- **Easy**: the system is intentionally dumb and often chooses weak moves.
- **Medium**: the system mixes smart moves with bad moves.
- **Hard**: the system almost always chooses the strongest move.

The level can be changed anytime. Changing the level starts a fresh round if a side is already selected.

## Players

- You control the symbol you selected.
- The rival is the system.
- You and the system take turns placing marks.

## Controls

- Tap any empty square to place your mark.
- Wait while the system chooses its move.
- Use **Next Round** to clear the board and keep the current scores.
- Use **Reset Scores** to clear the board and reset all scores to zero.

## Score Section

The colorful score section tracks the score for X, draws, and O.

- **You X** or **System X**
- **Draws**
- **You O** or **System O**

Scores stay visible between rounds until you press **Reset Scores** or choose a side again.

## Round Results

- If your symbol gets three in a row, you win and your score increases.
- If the system's symbol gets three in a row, the system wins and its score increases.
- If all nine squares are filled without a winner, the round is a draw.

## Tips

- Try to control the center tile.
- Watch for diagonal threats.
- Block the system before setting up your own win.
