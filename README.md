# How To Play

## Goal

Win the round by placing three of your marks in a row before the other player does.

You can win with:

- Three marks across a row
- Three marks down a column
- Three marks diagonally

## Players

- Player X always starts each round.
- Player O plays after Player X.
- Players take turns tapping empty tiles.

## Controls

- Tap any empty square to place your mark.
- Use **Next Round** to clear the board and keep the current scores.
- Use **Reset Scores** to clear the board and reset all scores to zero.

## Score Section

The colorful score section tracks:

- **Player X** wins
- **Draws**
- **Player O** wins

Scores stay visible between rounds until you press **Reset Scores**.

## Round Results

- If Player X gets three in a row, Player X wins and their score increases.
- If Player O gets three in a row, Player O wins and their score increases.
- If all nine squares are filled without a winner, the round is a draw.

## Tips

- Try to control the center tile.
- Watch for diagonal threats.
- Block your opponent before setting up your own win.
